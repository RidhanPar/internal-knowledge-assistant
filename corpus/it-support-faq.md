# IT Support FAQ

Answers to the most common IT and workplace questions. For anything not covered
here, contact the IT service desk in the #it-help channel or at
it@meridian.example.

## How do I get IT support?

Post in #it-help for quick questions, or raise a ticket in the service portal for
anything that needs tracking (hardware faults, access requests, software
installs). The service desk is staffed 08:00–18:00 UK time on working days.
Urgent issues affecting production are handled through the on-call process, not
the service desk.

## I forgot my password or I'm locked out. What do I do?

Use the self-service password reset in the identity portal, which requires your
registered MFA device. If you have lost your MFA device as well, contact the IT
service desk to verify your identity and re-enrol; the service desk will never
ask you for your password.

## I lost my laptop or phone. What now?

Report it immediately as a security incident via #security-incidents, then raise
an IT ticket for a replacement. Company laptops are encrypted and can be remotely
wiped, so prompt reporting protects company data. Do not wait until the next
working day.

## Can I install software myself?

You may install software from the approved self-service catalogue without a
ticket. Anything outside the catalogue requires an IT ticket so that licensing
and security can be checked. Do not install software from unverified sources, and
never disable the endpoint security agent.

## How do I request new hardware?

Raise a ticket in the service portal describing what you need and why. Standard
peripherals (monitors, keyboards, headsets) are usually approved within two
working days. Higher-value items follow the approval thresholds in the Expense
and Travel Policy.

## How do I raise an ethics or conduct concern?

Concerns about conduct, harassment, or ethics can be raised with your manager or
People Operations, or anonymously through the ethics line at
ethics@meridian.example or the anonymous web form linked in the portal. Reports
are handled confidentially and retaliation for raising a concern in good faith is
itself a serious policy violation.

## What is the guest Wi-Fi policy for the hubs?

Visitors may use the guest Wi-Fi network, which is isolated from Meridian
systems. Never connect a visitor's device to the corporate network, and do not
share the corporate Wi-Fi credentials with anyone outside Meridian.
