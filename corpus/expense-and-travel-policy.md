# Expense and Travel Policy

This policy governs how Meridian employees spend company money on travel,
equipment, and day-to-day expenses. The guiding principle is to spend company
money as if it were your own, and to keep receipts.

## General Principles

All expenses must be reasonable, necessary for your work, and supported by an
itemised receipt. Submit expenses through the finance portal within 30 days of
incurring them. Expenses submitted more than 60 days late may not be reimbursed.
Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run after approval.

## Approval Thresholds

- Expenses up to £100 (or local equivalent) require no pre-approval.
- Expenses between £100 and £1,000 require manager approval before purchase.
- Expenses above £1,000 require both manager and department-head approval.

Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is a policy violation.

## Meals and Subsistence

When travelling for work, you may claim actual meal costs up to a daily cap of
£60 in the UK and Europe and $80 in the US, inclusive of drinks and tips.
Alcohol is reimbursable only for client entertainment, not for individual meals.
No receipt is required for individual items under £10, but the daily cap still
applies.

## Travel

Book travel through the corporate travel tool where possible. Economy class is
standard for flights under six hours; premium economy is permitted for flights
over six hours. Rail travel is preferred over flying for journeys under four
hours where a rail option exists. Choose standard-class rail unless a first-class
fare is cheaper.

Personal travel may be combined with business travel, but Meridian pays only the
portion that would have been incurred for the business trip alone.

## Home Office and Equipment

Every employee receives a one-time home-office allowance of £500 to set up their
workspace, plus a company laptop. Additional equipment (monitors, peripherals)
is provided on request through IT and remains company property. Software licences
for work must be requested through IT rather than expensed directly, so that
security and licensing are managed centrally.

## Professional Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of £1,500 for courses, conferences,
and books that support their role. Conference travel counts against the travel
policy above, not the learning budget. Unused learning budget does not carry over
between years.
