# Meridian Employee Handbook

This handbook summarises the core policies that apply to all Meridian employees.
Where a policy references a more detailed document (for example the Security
Policy or the Expense & Travel Policy), that document takes precedence.

## Working Hours and Flexibility

Meridian operates a flexible working model. Core collaboration hours are 10:00
to 16:00 in your local time zone, during which you are expected to be reachable
for meetings and pairing. Outside core hours you may arrange your schedule to
suit your work and personal commitments.

The standard working week is 37.5 hours. Employees are not expected to work
evenings or weekends; if a project requires it, time off in lieu is granted at
the manager's discretion.

## Remote and Hybrid Work

All roles are remote-first. Employees may work fully remotely, or use one of the
regional hubs in London, Berlin, or Austin. There is no mandated number of
office days. If you work from a location outside your country of employment for
more than 30 consecutive days, you must notify People Operations in advance for
tax and compliance reasons.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to public holidays in their country of employment. Leave accrues
monthly and may be taken as it accrues. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over
into the following year; days beyond that are forfeited unless carry-over is
approved by a manager in exceptional circumstances.

Requests for annual leave should be submitted at least two weeks in advance for
absences longer than three days. There is no cap on the number of consecutive
days you may request, subject to manager approval and team cover.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year at full pay. Sick
leave does not need to be certified for absences of three days or fewer. For
absences of four or more consecutive days, a medical note is required. Sick
leave is separate from annual leave and does not reduce your holiday balance.

## Parental Leave

Meridian offers 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave to the primary caregiver
and 8 weeks of fully paid leave to the secondary caregiver, regardless of
gender. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
Employees on parental leave continue to accrue annual leave.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with
respect. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying are grounds for disciplinary
action up to and including termination. Concerns may be raised confidentially
with your manager, with People Operations, or through the anonymous ethics line
described in the IT Support FAQ.

## Probation Period

New employees serve a probation period of six months, during which either party
may end the employment relationship with two weeks' notice. On successful
completion of probation, the standard notice period is one month for individual
contributors and three months for senior leadership.
